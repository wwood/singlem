# SingleM prokaryotic fraction

The SingleM prokaryotic fraction, or SPF, estimates what percentage of metagenomic sequence derives from Bacteria and Archaea, including their plasmids. The remaining sequence may derive from hosts, other eukaryotes, phages, or material outside that definition.

SPF combines lineage coverage from a taxonomic profile with estimated genome sizes. This avoids requiring reference genomes for every non-prokaryotic organism in the sample.

The estimate is least certain when a simple community is dominated by novel lineages whose genome sizes are poorly known. Multi-copy plasmids can also cause underestimation because reference genome sizes count each plasmid once.
